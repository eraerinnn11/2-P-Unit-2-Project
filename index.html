A Night In The Woods

Developed by: Infinite Fall and Secret Lab (2017)

About the game

A Night in the Woods is a 2D single player story game that is set in a zoomorphic world where you play as a feline 20 year old college dropout, Mae, who returned to her weird-feeling hometown, Possum Springs. Upon her return,she meets up with some of her childhood friends (and bandmates) who help her get re-established in town. The game begins Mae doing random tasks like meeting up with these friends and going to parties. However, she starts getting these really weird dreams with no way to wake up until she finishes tasks.
