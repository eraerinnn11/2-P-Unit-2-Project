A Night In The Woods

Developed by: Infinite Fall and Secret Lab (2017)

About the game

A Night in the Woods is a 2D single player story game that is set in a zoomorphic world where you play as a feline 20 year old college dropout, Mae, who returned to her weird-feeling hometown, Possum Springs. Upon her return, she meets up with some of her childhood friends (and bandmates) who help her get re-established in town. The game begins Mae doing random tasks like meeting up with these friends and going to parties. However, she starts getting these really weird dreams with no way to wake up until she finishes tasks. Further into the game, things start to get weird, whenever she ventures out into the woods, she sees this being following her. She calls it the ghost. Everytime she sees it, she attempts to confront it but it never answers and just runs off, until one time, where she gets the answers she's been searching for.

Main Characters

Mae

Mae is the main protaganist of the game and who you play as (most of the time), she is a . She is very energetic and impulsive. She jumps on cars, mail boxes, she can get up to the roofs of the houses in her hometown, she'll pretty much climb anything she can. She has a troubled past of depression and anger issues, and can be referred to as disrespectful by most of the elders in her town. Throughout the game, she gets herself into disappointing and, towards the end, dangerous situations, by saying the wrong things to her friends and giving into her reckless side. She doesn't always mean to do the things she does, so she always tries to make up for it.

Bea

Bea is Mae's best friend and the bassist of their band. She is very serious and responsible, and is the one who usually has to clean up after Mae's messes. She is also very protective of her friends, and will do anything to help them out. She has a troubled past as well, and has a strained relationship with her father. Throughout the game, she tries to help Mae get back on track and make better decisions, but sometimes her seriousness can come off as cold or uncaring.

Gregg

Gregg is Mae's childhood friend and the drummer of their band. The two would go around town doing somewhat illegal acts when they were in high school.
